Meridian Components operates a critical inventory dashboard that its operations team depends on daily — and right now, that system is holding the business back. The Reports module has known defects that remain unresolved. The Tokyo warehouse team works in English-only views. IT has blocked further changes because there is no test coverage. And the operations team lacks a restocking tool that could meaningfully reduce carrying costs and stockout risk across three warehouses.

We understand the core challenge: the previous vendor left the system functional but incomplete, with thin documentation and no tests. Any new vendor needs to first establish a solid foundation — fix what's broken, establish test coverage so IT can safely approve changes — before extending the platform. That is exactly how we propose to sequence this engagement.

Our approach:

1. **Remediate first.** We will audit and resolve all defects in the Reports module (R1), beginning in week one. This delivers immediate value to the operations team and unblocks further work.
2. **Establish test coverage in parallel.** End-to-end browser tests (R3) will be built alongside remediation so that every fix is covered from the start — not added as an afterthought.
3. **Build the Restocking feature on a proven base.** Once the foundation is stable, we will deliver the Restocking recommendations view (R2) that VP Operations Tanaka's team has been waiting for: purchase order recommendations driven by current stock levels, demand forecasts, and an operator-supplied budget ceiling.
4. **Document for handoff.** A current-state architecture overview (R4) will be delivered at engagement close, written for Meridian IT — not for developers.

Desired enhancements (UI modernization, expanded i18n for Tokyo staff, dark mode for warehouse floor stations) will be scoped as stretch deliverables once the required work is underway.

We are committing to a fixed-fee engagement staffed by two senior engineers over 10 weeks, with milestone-based payments so Meridian has full cost predictability. Timeline and pricing details follow in subsequent sections.
