R2 — Restocking Recommendations

The feature your ops team
has been waiting for.

The Restocking view will give your operations team automated purchase order recommendations — no more manual spreadsheet math.

Inputs: current stock levels, demand forecast, and an operator-supplied budget ceiling. Output: a prioritized list of what to reorder, how much, and from which warehouse.

Key inputs

· Current stock per SKU per warehouse
· Demand forecast (existing API)
· Operator budget ceiling
· Reorder lead times

Key outputs

· Recommended purchase orders
· Priority ranking by stockout risk
· Budget utilization summary

R3 — Automated Testing

Tests that unblock your IT team.
